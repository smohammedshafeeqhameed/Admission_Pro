Frequent Questions

How do I track my active referrals?
Simply navigate to your Executive Hub Dashboard. All candidate activities are logged in the "Enrollment Stream" in real-time. You can filter by institution, course, or status.
When are commission payments processed?
Payouts are typically processed twice a month (10th and 25th) after candidates complete their registration fee payment and initial document verification by the college manager.
Can I refer to multiple colleges simultaneously?
Absolutely. Once your profile is approved, you can share tracking links for any allocated institution in your portfolio. All data merges seamlessly into your single dashboard view.
My candidate link isn't opening, what should I do?
First, ensure you've copied the full URL from your Referral Engine sidebar. If the issue persists, clear your browser cache or contact your dedicated success manager via the "Contact Hub" button on your dashboard.
