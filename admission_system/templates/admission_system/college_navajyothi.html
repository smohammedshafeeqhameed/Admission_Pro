Lighted to enlighten

A campus built for growth

A well-stocked library, a computer lab, a reprographic center and a career training center sit alongside the college canteen — a campus built for both study and placement readiness.

Run by the CST Fathers

An academic initiative of the Little Flower Congregation's CST Fathers, recognising a community's yearning for higher education and answering it with a values-driven campus.

Five degree programmes
